## 中文

- **局域网模型服务可以用了**：另一台电脑或 NAS 上的 `http://192.168.x.x` 等内网地址，以前能通过 Key 检查却一直没切换过去，翻译还在用原来的服务。现在本机和局域网地址都可以用 http；公网地址仍然必须 https。
- 服务没切换过去时，设置里会说清卡在哪：地址不能用、还缺 Key、还没选模型，或者还没登录 ChatGPT。
- 书上的报错分开说：服务还没有 Key，还是钥匙串没解锁。

之前碰到这个问题的话：在设置 › 翻译服务里重新选「自定义服务」并填一次地址，看到「已保存」后，对那本书点「重试」。

## English

- **Model servers on your local network work now**: an `http://192.168.x.x` address on another computer or a NAS passed the key check but never took effect, so translation kept using the old service. Addresses on this Mac or your local network may now use http; public addresses still need https.
- When a service has not taken over yet, Settings says what is missing: a usable address, a working key, a model, or a ChatGPT sign-in.
- Book errors now tell apart a service with no key from a locked Keychain.

If you hit this before: choose Custom again in Settings › Translation Service and enter the address once; when it shows Saved, press Retry on the book.
