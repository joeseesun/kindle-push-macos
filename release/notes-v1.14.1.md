## 中文

- **一眼看出哪台 Kindle 的接收端是旧版，并能直接用 USB 升级**：连接面板说明旧版发不了哪些格式，点一下开始升级，不用重新配对；工具栏也有小标记。
- 修复：旧版 Kindle 插上 USB 时显示「已写入」，只能推出，没法升级。现在会显示「升级」。
- Kindle 插着线但还在 KOReader 里时，直接提示「线已经连上了，还差退出 KOReader」，不再让人反复插线。

## English

- See which Kindle has an old receiver and upgrade it over USB: the connection panel says which formats it can't take and starts the upgrade, with no new pairing. The toolbar shows a mark too.
- Fixed: a Kindle with an old receiver read as "written" over USB and could only be ejected. It can now be upgraded.
- A Kindle on the cable but still in KOReader is told so, instead of being asked to connect again.
