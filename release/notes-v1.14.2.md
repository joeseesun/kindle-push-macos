## 中文

- **修复整本译完却报「译本缺少分段文件」**：部分 EPUB（如 Snow Crash）会在最后一步失败。译文都保留着，升级后点「重试」即可，已译好的部分不重复计费。
- 失败的书行上有「重试」按钮，点了立刻开始，只处理这一本，发送还是保存跟上次一样。
- ⋯ 菜单和右键菜单新增「重新翻译…」：丢弃译文和进度从头翻，点之前会说明会再次计费。

## English

- Fixed books such as Snow Crash failing at the very end of translation with a missing-parts error. The translation is kept: update and press Retry; finished parts are not paid for again.
- Failed rows now have a Retry button that starts that one book at once, for the same destination as last time.
- "Translate Again…" in the row and context menus discards a translation and starts over, and says first that it costs usage again.
